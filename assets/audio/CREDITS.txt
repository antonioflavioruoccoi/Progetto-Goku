Dr. Goku — Original minigame music

Oak's Notebook (oak-notebook.m4a): 112 BPM, warm minor-key chiptune for
PokéIndizi's Professor Oak dialogue, clue reveals and Game Boy aesthetic.
Castelia Card Market (castelia-card-market.m4a): 132 BPM, bright syncopated
chiptune for PokéPrice's city backdrop and fast higher/lower comparisons.

Composed and synthesized for Dr. Goku with Codex, November 2026.
These original compositions use no samples, recordings or melodies from
Pokémon games. The franchise is an aesthetic reference only.
Both new tracks and scripts/music.py are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Use, modification and redistribution in this repository and on the website
are permitted without attribution. Existing music is outside this dedication.

Regenerate the PCM WAV loops: python3 scripts/music.py
Source: mono, 22050 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM. Distribution: AAC, 64 kbit/s.
Convert WAV output using macOS afconvert (repeat for each file):
afconvert -f m4af -d aac -b 64000 assets/audio/oak-notebook.wav assets/audio/oak-notebook.m4a
The website loads only local M4A files; no remote audio service is used.
